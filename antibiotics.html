Chart notes

  • Rifampin should not be used alone for Staphylococcal infections
  • Enterococci has two main species - Enterococcus faecalis and Enterococcus faecium; the antibiotics listed are active against E.faecalis, but have limited activity for E.faecium
  • Cephalosporins as well as beta-lactamase inhibitor combinations have in-vitro activity for HECK-YeS organisms but may induce production of beta-lactamases
  • Tigecycline is active against HECK-YeS organisms, but for Proteus spp it depends on local susceptibility data
  • SAP = Special Access Program (i.e., not marketed but available from Health Canada)
